## Notes

- `helicopter-helipad` is cropped (see `CROPS` in `scripts/optimize-images.mjs`) to remove a bystander's silhouette.
- The "before" side of every before/after slider is a filtered version of the "after" photo. It is labelled as an illustration on the page. Replace it with real before/after pairs of your own work.
- The logo, icons, OG image and all illustrations (aircraft plan view, map, flight path) are original SVG artwork made for this project.
